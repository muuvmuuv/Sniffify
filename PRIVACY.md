# Privacy Policy — Sniffify

**TL;DR: Sniffify collects nothing. Everything stays on your device.**

Last updated: July 21, 2026

## Data Collection

Sniffify collects, transmits, and shares **no data whatsoever**:

- **No account.** There is nothing to sign up for.
- **No tracking, no analytics, no ads.** The app contains no third-party
  SDKs, no analytics frameworks, and makes no network requests.
- **No servers.** There is no backend. Nothing leaves your device.

## What stays on your device

- **Profile inputs** (height, weight) and **session results** (line length,
  time, success) are stored locally on your device only, and can be erased
  at any time via *Einstellungen → Alles zurücksetzen*.
- **Microphone**: used exclusively during the game's countdown/pull window
  to detect a sharp inhale. Audio is analyzed on-device in real time and
  immediately discarded — it is never stored or transmitted. You can deny
  microphone access; the game then judges by touch alone.
- **Optional debug recordings**: if you explicitly enable
  *Einstellungen → Debug → Sniff-Aufnahmen speichern*, the pull window is
  saved as an audio file **into your local Documents folder** (visible in
  the Files app). These files never leave your device unless you share them
  yourself, and you can delete them in the app at any time.
- **Sharing**: the Wrapped recap can be exported as an image via the system
  share sheet — only when you tap share, only to the destination you choose.
